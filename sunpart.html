sunpart — disk partition layout planner

Describe your disk the way format reports it, lay out slices a–h in megabytes, and get back cylinder-aligned numbers you can type straight into the partition menu. Everything is packed on cylinder boundaries, so overlaps are impossible; the checks below tell you if the layout will boot and install.
